Save your parking spot and find your car

Save a parking location, floor or bay number, optional photo and GPS coordinates; keep an eye on parking time and fees.

Language availability: This guide is in English. The linked application has not yet been fully translated; some controls and account messages may appear in German. Some features require a free Toolya account.

Getting started

  1. Describe where you parked

    Sign in and enter the car park or street. In a multi-storey car park, record a floor, colour, sector or bay number, such as level −2, section C, bay 42.

  2. Add an optional photo or location

    Take a photo of a sign or bay number. When saving, your browser may ask for location permission. If you decline, the textual parking details and a selected photo can still be saved.

  3. Find the saved location

    Open your saved parking records. Use the notes and photo; if GPS coordinates were saved, the route action opens that location in Google Maps.

Parking-time reminders

The tool provides 30-minute, one-hour and two-hour timers. Check actual local restrictions and signage yourself: a timer does not extend your parking permission.

Parking fees and household expenses

Record a fee you actually paid. For supported positive amounts, you may choose to send the expense to the Toolya household budget through Toolya Connect.

When GPS is unavailable

A clear written note and photo are especially useful in underground car parks, where satellite positioning may be unreliable.

Frequently asked questions

Can I save a car park without GPS?

Yes. A description, floor and photo can still help you locate the car.

Does Toolya guarantee that a parking session is legally valid?

No. Always follow the parking signs and rules at the location.

Find your car in a multi-storey car park

Before leaving, note the car park, level, zone and bay, for example level −2, zone C, bay 42. A photo of the sign by the stairs can help you on the way back. You can use the description and photo without GPS. The route button appears on entries with coordinates.

Check the timer and export parking fees

A timer alert depends on the page being able to keep running. Browser notifications also depend on permission. Check the time yourself if you close the tab or the browser pauses it.

Review saved fees in your parking history and use the PDF report to keep a copy. If you transfer a paid fee to the household budget, save the parking entry first and confirm the transfer. Location, photos and parking notes stay with the original entry.
