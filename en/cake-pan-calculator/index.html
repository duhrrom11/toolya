Understand the area of a round pan

For round pans with a similar filling height, scale ingredient quantities using the ratio of their base areas: (new diameter ÷ original diameter)².

Example: 10-inch recipe into an 8-inch pan

The factor is (8 ÷ 10)² = 0.64. A recipe calling for 250 g flour would scale to about 160 g. Keep units consistent and check whether pan depth is comparable.

Check baking conditions separately

Changing a pan size may also change baking time and cake height. Monitor doneness rather than assuming time scales with ingredient quantities.
