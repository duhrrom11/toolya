Getting started

  1. Add your vehicle

    Sign in to Toolya, create a vehicle and give it a recognisable name. You can add a registration number or odometer reading if useful. Your vehicle becomes the starting point for later cost records.

  2. Record each expense

    Choose the vehicle, date, type of cost, description and amount. Fuel, repairs, insurance and vehicle tax are examples of expenses you can record. Attach a receipt when you have one.

  3. Review costs and maintenance

    Filter your expense history by vehicle. You can create a PDF report and keep track of upcoming inspections and tyre changes.

Keep a reliable fuel log

Enter the odometer reading and fuel information consistently. A meaningful consumption comparison requires at least two relevant fill-up records; a single stop does not establish an average.

Understand cost per kilometre

Divide recorded vehicle expenses by distance travelled over the same period. The result is an estimate based on your entries, not a full ownership-cost forecast if some expenses are missing.

Optional Toolya Connect

You can opt to transfer an eligible vehicle expense to the Toolya household budget when saving it. Set up the household budget in the same Toolya account first. Confirm the transfer after saving the vehicle entry. Check the payment method in the household budget, where it initially appears as cash. Receipt attachments stay with the original entry.

Record maintenance and export appointments

Save dated entries for inspections, servicing and tyre changes. For eligible entries, use the calendar download to save an ICS file and import it into your calendar. Use the tyre section to keep a separate record of your tyre sets.

For example, €120 in fixed costs, €90 in fuel and €40 in maintenance add up to €250. Over 800 km, that is about €0.31 per kilometre. Use the costs and distance from the same period for each vehicle.

Frequently asked questions

Can I manage more than one car?

Yes. Give each vehicle a unique name and select the correct vehicle when recording an expense.

Does Toolya automatically calculate costs I never entered?

No. The overview reflects the data you choose to record.
