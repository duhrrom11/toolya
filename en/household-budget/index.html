Getting started

  1. Record your first expense

    Sign in, choose an expense, then enter its name, date, amount and category. For example, a grocery shop of €42.80 may belong to the food category.

  2. Add income and monthly budgets

    Use the income option for salary or refunds. Set a monthly budget for a spending category and compare it with the expenses already entered.

  3. Find transactions and set savings goals

    Filter your transactions to review previous spending. You can also create a savings goal with a target amount for a planned purchase.

Receipt photographs are optional

You can record a transaction manually or use a receipt photo to assist input. Review the recognised information before saving; a photo should not be treated as a guaranteed accurate entry.

Budget versus savings goal

A monthly budget limits or monitors a category of spending over a period. A savings goal is a target amount you want to put aside, such as a deposit or holiday.

Connect other Toolya tools

For supported expenses in the car, parking or pet-health tools, you may optionally transfer an entry into the household budget after saving the original. Check the transfer confirmation.

Reconcile your monthly budget

With a €300 food budget, €241 in purchases and a €12 refund, your net spending is €229 and €71 remains. Subtract the refund only if it has yet to be included in your spending total.

Use Toolya to keep transactions, categories, receipt photos and budgets together. A spreadsheet is useful when you want to set up your own columns and formulas.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to attach receipts?

No. Manual entry is supported.

Will an external expense appear automatically?

Only when the relevant Toolya Connect option is chosen and the transfer is confirmed.

If a save is not confirmed

Write down the transaction name, date and amount. When your connection is stable, reload and check the transaction history. Enter it again only if it is missing.
