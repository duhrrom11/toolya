CHAPTER 9

To be continued

By Romi (Romain Duhr)

A personal look at Toolya’s future, new ideas and the dream of a simple life somewhere sunny.

My next big goal is retirement. My partner and I hope to move somewhere sunny in the south. No luxury, just a simple life. As long as it’s warm. A sofa, coffee and internet would be useful, too.

Toolya keeps evolving. I want to share new ideas, genuine mishaps and small victories here. Perhaps you’ll recognise yourself in some of these stories. Thanks for coming along for the ride. – Romi
