CHAPTER 7

The Yogasutra development method

By Romi (Romain Duhr)

A hobby developer reflects on AI-assisted coding, learning from mistakes and working from the sofa.

I work on a laptop or tablet on the sofa. After a few hours I sometimes find myself in a position somewhere between yoga and the Kama Sutra. My partner gives me time for myself and occasionally untangles me. Meanwhile my legs and feet keep moving. Apparently I find that relaxing.

AI helps me develop. Sometimes neither of us seems capable of understanding what the other wants. Still, it’s fun. My motto: It’s not a bug, it’s a feature! Of course the tools should work reliably, and I take real bugs seriously. Toolya isn’t perfect. But it has heart and soul. I want you to feel that.
