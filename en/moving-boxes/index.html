📦 moving box organizer

Organize moving boxes with QR labels

Keep a clear inventory of what goes into each moving box. Label boxes, find items faster and make unpacking less stressful.

Language notice: This guide is in English. The linked application offers a partial English interface. Some controls, account steps and messages still appear in German. Some features require a free account.

Get started in three steps

  1. Create a box and choose a location.
  2. Add item descriptions before sealing the box.
  3. Print labels and update the inventory while moving.

Label every box before you move

Assign each box a short code such as KITCHEN-01, BEDROOM-02 or STORAGE-03. Record its room and a short list of contents. Consistent box labels make searching much easier.

Create an inventory you can actually use

Keep descriptions specific: “coffee maker and filters” is more useful than “kitchen stuff”. Update locations when boxes move from a van to a storage room.

Find things again after moving

Look up the item you need in your inventory, identify its box and then check the label. Toolya supports managing boxes and generating QR labels from the tool interface.

Plan the move room by room

In the moving planner, set up the rooms at your new address and assign boxes to them. Use the arrival overview to see which boxes have arrived and which still need unpacking.

Print QR labels and share transport updates

Export the QR labels as a PDF and print a test page before labelling every box. You can also find a box by searching its name or contents.

In Transport & helpers, create a time-limited helper link that you can revoke later. Share it only with people you trust. Helpers can update transport status and see box names and destination rooms. Your inventory contents stay private.

Check that your changes are saved

If Toolya cannot confirm a save, check your connection and wait for confirmation before closing the app. After reopening it, check that your latest changes are there.
