Getting started

  1. Choose the correct meter type

    Sign in, add a reading and choose electricity, gas, cold water or hot water. If your electricity meter has two tariffs, record the high and low tariffs separately.

  2. Identify the meter clearly

    Enter the meter name or serial number and use the same identifier each time. Optionally include the meter location so similar meters are not mixed up.

  3. Enter the date and displayed number

    Record the reading exactly as displayed, with an optional photo or note. Review the history to compare it with the previous reading from the same meter.

Reading versus consumption

A reading is the cumulative number shown by a meter. Consumption over a period is generally the later reading minus the earlier one for that same meter, subject to resets or replacements.

Example

If a meter shows 12,450 kWh on one date and 12,520 kWh later, the difference is 70 kWh. Keep the meter identifier and units consistent.

Moving and property handover

You can prepare a PDF handover record with the readings documented at the time of the move. A meter reading does not automatically notify an energy supplier.

Frequently asked questions

Why is no consumption difference shown yet?

At least two compatible readings for the same meter are needed.

Does Toolya send readings to my supplier?

No. Submit any required readings through your provider's official channel.

Record a meter replacement

Record the final reading and number of the old meter and the first reading and number of the new one as separate entries. Compare readings only from the same meter. For example, if the old meter had previously read 12,520 kWh and its final reading is 12,580 kWh, the difference is 60 kWh.

Estimate gas consumption in kilowatt-hours

Subtract the earlier reading from the current one, then multiply the result in cubic metres by the conversion factor on your bill. For example, 16 m³ at 10.5 kWh/m³ gives 168 kWh. Use your supplier's own factor for your estimate.
