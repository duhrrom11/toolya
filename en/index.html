Frequently asked questions

How much does Toolya cost?

The tools currently available are free to use. Voluntary support via Ko-fi is welcome. Some product links in the wish list are affiliate links.

Do I need an account?

Most tools require a Toolya account. Public wish lists can be browsed without signing in.

Where is my data stored?

Depending on the tool, data is stored in your browser and/or with an online service such as Supabase. Some features use other external services. See our Privacy policy.

How do I back up my data?

Depending on the tool, settings include backup and restore features. The home page backup includes supported local browser data only, not a full backup of cloud data. Encrypt confidential backups.

What does the spinning gear do?

It opens settings for your account, backup and appearance. Additional tool settings are available where needed.

Can I open my tools faster?

Tap the star on a tool card. Find your favourite tools under Your favourites in the dashboard.
