Get started in three steps

  1. Add your plants and their locations.
  2. Record watering and other care activities.
  3. Use your history to adapt the routine.

Create a profile for each plant

Give each plant a name and note its location. Different rooms and seasons can change the care it needs.

Track care instead of guessing

Record watering and care notes over time. This helps you spot patterns without assuming every plant needs the same fixed schedule.

Use reminders responsibly

A reminder is a prompt to check the plant, not proof it needs water. Check the soil, light and plant condition first.

Change a watering schedule

Toolya adds the interval you enter to the last watering date. For example, if you water on 3 May and set an interval of five days, the next check is due on 8 May. Check the soil first. If you decide to wait, leave the last watering date as it is. When you do water, record it to update the schedule.

Use the plant card to edit the interval or check the last recorded date. If several plants share a location, use the location filter to find them while keeping a separate schedule for each.

Keep a plant diary and track supplies

Add dated notes for repotting, fertilising or propagation. Use photos to compare growth over time. You can also record harvest weight in grams and note the variety and harvest year in your seed inventory.

If you track a water tank, logging a watering subtracts the estimated amount from the recorded supply. The estimate uses the selected pot size. Check the soil and adjust how much you water to suit the plant and its location.
