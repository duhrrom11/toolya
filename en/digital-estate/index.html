Getting started

  1. Open the vault with your Toolya account

    Sign in and open the digital vault. By default, the account password also protects the encrypted backup. You may set a separate vault password in its settings.

  2. Add an important record

    Create a record with a descriptive title and category. Write down what trusted people should know about a contract, subscription or digital account. Small supporting files can be added when needed.

  3. Prepare trusted-person access

    Add trusted contacts and review the backup before distributing their personal keys. Keep each key with its intended person.

Two-key access

The guide describes a shared read-only access path in which two different trusted contacts combine their keys. Do not assume a single key provides the same access.

Emergency alerts and check-ins

Follow the tool's instructions for emergency access and for stopping an alert with a check-in. Regular reminder notifications are not themselves proof that an emergency has occurred.

Handle recovery information carefully

Store recovery details safely and verify that you can access your own encrypted backup before relying on this as part of an estate plan. Toolya is not a substitute for legal advice or formal estate documentation.

Frequently asked questions

Can I add trusted contacts?

Yes, the tool supports personal trusted contacts. Review what access each method grants before handing out keys.

Does my emergency contact get unrestricted access immediately?

No. Different access methods and conditions apply; follow the vault's instructions.

Check the backup before handing over keys

Save an online backup and use the backup check in the vault settings. Check each trusted person's name and email address. Give each person only their own key and the trusted-person access link. Anyone holding two different keys can open the vault for reading without another person present.

For an attachment, use a photo or document up to 250 KB. Keep passwords and keys separate from your handover notes.

Understand the emergency waiting period

One trusted person can report an emergency using their key. Toolya sends the owner an email asking them to check in. Once the waiting period begins, the alert status shows when access may become available. After 24 hours without an all-clear, one personal key can grant read access.

The owner can check in from the vault and confirm with their account password. New access then requires two keys again. This does not remove copies that recipients have already saved.
