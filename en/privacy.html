Privacy notice

English translation of the German original.

1. Privacy at a glance

We take the protection of your personal data seriously. Toolya's free online tools and calculators follow the principles of data minimisation and privacy by design. Many functions save information directly in your browser; others use Supabase, Firebase or external services. The data flows vary by tool. You decide what information to enter in public wish lists.

2. Hosting through GitHub Pages

The site is hosted using GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P Kelly Jr St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA.

When you access the website, GitHub automatically collects technical server logs such as IP address, access date and time, browser type and operating system. These are technically required to deliver the website. See GitHub’s privacy statement for further details.

3. Browser local storage

Many tools save entries such as inventory lists and household budget transactions in browser local storage. Some features can also use external services. Whether and what data is transmitted depends on the tool.

Local entries can be edited or deleted in many tools. Backups cover only the local data supported by that tool. Deleting browser data does not automatically remove information already sent to the cloud.

4. Accounts and online services

Supabase Auth is used for signing in from the homepage. Individual tools, particularly the digital vault, also use Google Firebase / Cloud Firestore. Data processing depends on each function; not every tool automatically synchronises.

Homepage sign-in is optional; some features require separate sign-in or setup. Communication with these services takes place over HTTPS; this is not equivalent to end-to-end encryption of all content.

Supabase Auth uses a Supabase project; see Supabase Privacy. Google services are provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland; see Google’s privacy policy.

5. External services, libraries, Ko-fi and Amazon affiliate links

JavaScript libraries may be loaded from third-party CDNs such as Tailwind CSS, jsDelivr or Google Firebase. Technical connection information, including IP addresses, may be sent to those providers. Some tools may use further services such as EmailJS.

We sometimes link to Ko-fi (Ko-fi.com) for voluntary donations. Clicking that link leaves our website and Ko-fi's privacy terms apply. We do not collect or store payment or credit card details ourselves.

Amazon affiliate links: As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Some tools or searches may show products and links to Amazon.de, clearly identified as advertising links. A qualifying purchase may earn us a commission without extra cost to you. Amazon may set cookies or collect tracking data when the link is opened; we do not control that.

6. Public wish lists, sharing and published content

The Amazon wish-list search is a public profile directory. All saved wish profiles are public; no private profiles are available. Display name, country, optional location, profile text, interests and up to five named Amazon wish lists may be publicly displayed, visited, shared and indexed by search engines. The form explains this before submission. Account sign-in email addresses and passwords are not published in wish profiles. Account holders can edit or delete their profiles.

Amazon wish lists are linked directly and are no longer imported for this search. All Amazon links include affiliate tag duhrrom-21. Amazon's privacy rules apply when visitors open or search Amazon; product images may load from Amazon servers. Product ideas are suggested from publicly provided interests and profile text. Bookmarked profiles are saved in the current browser.

If personal estate keys are sent by email or WhatsApp, the chosen service receives the message containing the key. Keys already shared, and downloaded files, cannot reliably be recalled.

7. Backups, deletion and retention

Local backups contain only supported browser data from the respective tool. Unencrypted backup files may reveal personal information. Deleting data in a browser does not automatically delete data stored in an online account. For deletion of accounts or server-side data, contact us unless the tool provides a deletion function. Retention periods and server-side logging may vary between providers.

8. Your rights

Where information is stored exclusively locally, we generally have no access to it. Under applicable law, you have rights to information, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. You may withdraw consent with future effect. You may complain to a competent data protection supervisory authority. Use the contact information below for online-data deletion requests.

9. Controller

Responsible for the content and delivery of Toolya's free tools and calculators: Romain Duhr. For privacy enquiries, email info@toolya.app.

10. Optional Google Analytics 4 usage statistics

With your explicit consent we use Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) to analyse page views and website use. The legal basis is your consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and, where applicable, Section 25(1) TDDDG. Without consent the Google Analytics script is not loaded.

With consent, page views, interactions, technical browser data, approximate location and online identifiers may be processed, potentially outside the EU. Retention depends on settings of our Google Analytics property. We do not use Analytics for personalised advertising.

You can change your decision any time using the “Cookie settings” link in the footer and withdraw consent for future processing. This prevents further Analytics collection on the site but does not retroactively delete previously transmitted data. See Google’s privacy policy.
