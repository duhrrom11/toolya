Get started in three steps

  1. Agree on which expenses are shared.
  2. Record costs, participants and repayments.
  3. Maintain a clear cleaning or task schedule.

Record shared expenses consistently

Agree which purchases count as shared expenses: rent, internet, cleaning products or groceries. Add the person who paid and keep individual spending separate.

Work out who owes what

For example, three roommates split a €60 shared purchase equally. Each owes €20; the person who paid is owed €40 by the others. Use the organizer to keep an understandable record of repayments.

Keep chores on the same page

Use a shared plan with clear responsibilities rather than relying on scattered chat messages. Revisit it whenever people move in or out.
