🏠 roommate expense tracker

Split roommate expenses and organize shared tasks

Stay on top of shared household spending, balances and chores with a practical organizer for roommates.

Get started in three steps

  1. Agree on which expenses are shared.
  2. Record costs, participants and repayments.
  3. Maintain a clear cleaning or task schedule.

Record shared expenses consistently

Agree which purchases count as shared expenses: rent, internet, cleaning products or groceries. Add the person who paid and keep individual spending separate.

Work out who owes what

For example, three roommates split a €60 shared purchase equally. Each owes €20; the person who paid is owed €40 by the others. Use the organizer to keep an understandable record of repayments.

Keep chores on the same page

Use a shared plan with clear responsibilities rather than relying on scattered chat messages. Revisit it whenever people move in or out.

Split an expense between selected roommates

Record who paid and select everyone who should share the cost, including the payer if they took part. If you leave the selection empty, Toolya uses all listed members. For example, if Lea pays €30 for herself and Sam, each share is €15. Sam owes Lea €15 and Kim has no share in that purchase.

Review the proposed repayments against the household's overall balance. You and your roommates make the transfers yourselves.

Set up your household, shopping list and calendar

Sign in and create a private household, then add the people you want to select for expenses or chores. Adding a contact does not give that person access. A new private household is initially accessible to the account that created it.

Use the shopping list for things your household still needs and the calendar for events or laundry slots. If you scan a receipt, compare the detected amount and date with the original before saving.
